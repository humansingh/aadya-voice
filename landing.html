The problem is often not eligibility. It is the interface.

Millions of useful opportunities are hidden inside English-heavy websites, long PDFs, unfamiliar forms and offices that are expensive to visit twice. Aadya turns that maze into a conversation: one question in a person’s mother tongue, one sourced answer, and one concrete next step.

Long-running voice agents can become an access layer for people who transact with the world through a phone and their mother tongue. It is not a replacement for the official office, but a better way to arrive prepared.
